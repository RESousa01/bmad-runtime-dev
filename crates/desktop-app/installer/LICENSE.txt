2. WHAT THIS INSTALLER DOES

Forge installs for the current user only. It does not require
administrator rights and it installs under your own user profile.

The Microsoft Edge WebView2 runtime is included inside this installer
rather than downloaded, so Forge can be installed on a machine with no
internet connection.


3. WHAT FORGE KEEPS ON THIS DEVICE

Forge keeps an encrypted local store in your user profile, under
AppData\Local. That store holds your conversations, your approval
decisions, capability results, and the list of workspace folders you
have granted Forge access to. It stays on this device.

Forge does not change a workspace folder unless you enable governed
edits for that folder and approve the specific change it proposes.


4. WHAT UNINSTALLING DOES

Uninstalling removes the program and leaves your local store in place,
so that reinstalling restores your history. The uninstaller offers a
clearly labelled option to erase the local store instead. That erasure
is permanent and cannot be undone.

You can also erase the local store at any time from inside Forge, under
Settings > Local data.

Neither path ever deletes or modifies your own workspace files.
